# Write a Research Paper

Deliver the requested manuscript or substantive revision from traceable evidence. Match language, venue, format and scope. Preserve authorship and source provenance; reconstruction of a published study is not new research.

## Establish the argument

Use the research question, method, actual experiments/proofs, primary and negative results, limitations and verified references. Distinguish observation, derivation, theorem, interpretation and open question. Inspect relevant original definitions or results when sources conflict; retain unresolved disagreement. Missing material in a writing packet does not prove it is absent from the study.

Explain the concrete problem, contribution, mechanism and corresponding evidence. Position it against verified related work without inferring novelty from missing literature. Use strong papers to learn argument structure, not to copy wording or impose their section layout. Abstract and conclusion must agree with the body.

Choose sections for the research type and available evidence. Use [academic-humanizer](../academic-humanizer/SKILL.md) for all manuscript language polishing, including a draft written during this task; do not add a separate house style. Keep provenance notes and unresolved author queries outside manuscript prose where the requested format permits. Do not invent a supplement, experiment, implementation detail, proof, citation or favorable conclusion to fill a gap.

## Preserve meaning

Before combining results, check matching model/variant, split, budget, resolution, training condition, units, metric and aggregation. A speed result does not inherit accuracy from a different operating point. Preserve numerical uncertainty, adverse results and relevant limitations.

Keep component names, dataflow, trained/frozen status, initialization and inference roles exact. For equations retain signs, denominators, index sets, assumptions and quantifiers; distinguish sufficient conditions from equivalence and empirical trends from proof. Use [scientific-fidelity.md](references/scientific-fidelity.md) for substantial quantitative, algorithmic or theoretical passages.

Organize results around scientific questions and matched comparisons. An ablation needs its corresponding control. Use smaller complete tables rather than invented or ambiguous cells; preserve comparators needed for each claim. Captions, axes and citations must match actual artifacts. Qualitative plots do not establish unstated numbers.

Use verified bibliography fields and sources that support their associated claims. Do not reconstruct paper details from memory or treat a reviewer score as scientific validation.

## Coordinate and finish

Keep scientific interpretation and acceptance with the current coordinating model. Only an explicit `/team`, `$team` or 混动 request activates [team](../team/SKILL.md); follow its current routing and send external workers only explicitly permitted material. An unavailable requested worker is a specific limitation, not permission to substitute another model.

Read and use [academic-humanizer](../academic-humanizer/SKILL.md) for language polishing. Use [format and fidelity guidance](../refine-text/references/document-writing.md) and the actual output-format skill only as needed. Check affected numbers, equations, tables, references, claim scope and rendering, reusing unchanged verification. Routine text or backup hashes are forbidden; hashing needs an actionable integrity, security or protocol-binding question. Resolve supported writing defects now; identify indispensable missing evidence without turning every gap into a new audit.

Return the manuscript plus concise material verification or author-query notes. Publication follows actual user authorization; neither polished prose nor compilation proves venue readiness.
