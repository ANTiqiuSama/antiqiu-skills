# Build Project Wiki

Maintain a compact navigation and synthesis layer over authoritative sources. Current code, tests, schemas, contracts and authorized runtime evidence take precedence over the Wiki.

## Choose the requested operation

- QUERY: answer through index, relevant page and necessary raw-source retrieval.
- AUDIT or PLAN: leave files unchanged; report concrete findings or proposed source-backed changes.
- APPLY: perform the authorized local update. “Update the Wiki” normally authorizes reversible edits without a separate plan approval.
- BUILD, MIGRATE or REBUILD: create, migrate or replace the structure; preserve rollback before replacement.

Use [wiki-migration-guide.md](references/wiki-migration-guide.md) for schemas, migration or complex cases. Read only needed sections: 2–4 for suitability/precedence, 5 and 9 for migration, 6 for complex queries, 7 for audits, 4.3 and 8 for shareable exports. A narrow edit needs no full guide or source inventory.

## Read and change

Identify the Wiki root, relevant sources and outcome. Read applicable instructions and the existing index; inspect manifests or startup markers only as needed. Verify uncertain, implementation-specific or consequential claims against raw sources and mark inference or unresolved conflict.

Accept an authorized source-linked research bundle as input without redoing literature discovery inside this skill. Exclude credentials, restricted/private material, inaccessible sources and generated outputs without knowledge value. The Wiki is not its own source set.

For local updates, preserve unrelated changes and revise existing pages before adding near-duplicates. Source disappearance triggers review/deprecation, not automatic deletion. Update affected index and metadata after page/link checks. Preserve surrounding Agent instructions and edit only an authorized marker block; a new Wiki startup block follows a successful pilot.

For rebuilds, resolve the exact target, keep a snapshot and verify representative questions. For queries, retrieve the smallest useful page set and follow links only for missing context. Distinguish sourced facts, inference, stale information and access gaps.

## Validate and deliver

Read and use [academic-humanizer](../academic-humanizer/SKILL.md) when polishing professional Wiki prose. Use [format and fidelity guidance](../refine-text/references/document-writing.md) only as needed. Check changed frontmatter, source locators, links/aliases, index/manifest agreement and evidence for important claims. Check relevant duplication, orphans, self-ingestion and sensitive content; representative questions should remain answerable. Do not hash edited pages or backups routinely; a hash must answer an actionable integrity, security or version-binding question. No fixed page count or token-saving multiplier proves quality.

Lead with the answer or changed content and useful source links. Report material checks/gaps, and rollback information for replacement. Keep audits read-only; existing authorization covers local repair only when repair is requested. External merge, publication or sharing follows its actual authorization.
